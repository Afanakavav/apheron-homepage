Board

The screen you present in Meet. It opens in a new tab: drag it to the second monitor.

Before the call

  1. Open Control here and Board in a new tab of the same Chrome, on the same address.
  2. Drag the Board tab to the second monitor and click it once: sound and full screen switch on.
  3. In Meet choose Present → A tab, pick “BOARD — Wheel of Facts” and keep “Also share tab audio” on.
  4. Put Meet next to Control on the laptop (a covered window runs slowly), keep the laptop plugged in and wear headphones.
